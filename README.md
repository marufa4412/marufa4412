👩‍💻 About Me

I'm a Business Analytics student at United International University who enjoys exploring what data can tell us about businesses, markets, and people.

My interests sit somewhere between analytics and research — from cleaning messy datasets and creating dashboards to exploring consumer behavior and asking research questions.

This GitHub is my space for documenting what I'm learning, what I'm building, and how my journey in analytics and research evolves over time.

📈 Visualization  → Turning numbers into understandable stories

🌱 Learning       → Continuously expanding my analytical toolkit
